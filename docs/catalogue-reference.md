# Veil of Light catalogue reference

Source: the user-supplied **To Breathe_FG Signature Catalogue.pdf**.

- Mediterranean interior moodboard: original page 19.
- Concrete Industrial interior moodboard: original page 20.
- Modern Tropical interior moodboard: original page 21.
- Warm beige stucco and natural brown terracotta exterior: page 15.
- White stucco, white travertine and dark charcoal shingle exterior: page 18.

The three interior boards and two exterior sheets are rendered from the original source pages. The viewer uses the Mediterranean exterior sheet for the Mediterranean scheme and the white/charcoal sheet for Concrete Industrial and Modern Tropical. Each scheme has a separately rebuilt native SketchUp furniture collection exported from its saved SKP: distinct sofas, chairs, tables, pendants, beds, vanities, shelving and home decor follow its original board. Source architecture, bathroom doors and built-in kitchen joinery remain at their original transforms. The new application sheets identify these as visual design studies. Products, construction build-ups, fabrication and clearances require coordinated drawings and specifications.

The original transparent Fjäll logo was reused from the supplied brand archive, with its transparent padding trimmed for display.

The furniture refinement follows the board silhouettes: deep loose ivory upholstery and wishbone timber dining chairs for Mediterranean; grey sectional seating with oak/steel chairs for Concrete Industrial; and open teak/cane frames, linen cushions and bent timber dining backs for Modern Tropical. Perimeter seams, relaxed cushions, draped throws, hollow ceramic profiles, books, trays and smaller planting replace the earlier simplified props.

The indoor study is on the enclosed loft floor, with the outdoor balcony named and checked separately against its original lower floor level.
