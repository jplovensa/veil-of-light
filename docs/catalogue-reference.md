Source: the user-supplied **To Breathe_FG Signature Catalogue.pdf**.

- Mediterranean interior moodboard: original page 19.
- Concrete Industrial interior moodboard: original page 20.
- Modern Tropical interior moodboard: original page 21.

The three interior boards and two exterior sheets are rendered from the original source pages. The viewer uses the Mediterranean exterior sheet for the Mediterranean scheme and the white/charcoal sheet for Concrete Industrial and Modern Tropical. Furniture geometry follows the furnished source model; material and pendant finish variants translate the catalogue direction onto that geometry. The new application sheets identify these as visual design studies. Products, construction build-ups, fabrication and clearances require coordinated drawings and specifications.

The original transparent Fjäll logo was reused from the supplied brand archive, with its transparent padding trimmed for display.
